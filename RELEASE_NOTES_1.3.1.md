FluentSidebar 1.3.1 — search fix

App search results now open toward the desktop instead of being cut off at the screen edge. The results panel keeps a readable width on either side of the desktop and follows the sidebar theme.

Result rows fit the panel, long app names wrap or show an ellipsis, and hovering shows the full name. Auto-hide waits while search results are open. Moving the sidebar closes the results before the rail changes sides.

The Windows taskbar remains visible. Existing notes and preferences are preserved.
