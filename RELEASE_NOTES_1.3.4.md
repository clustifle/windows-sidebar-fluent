FluentSidebar 1.3.4

Feed headlines can now show article thumbnails supplied by RSS and Atom feeds, including Media RSS images, image enclosures, and pictures in feed descriptions. Missing or unavailable pictures leave a text-only headline. Turn pictures off in the card's customization settings.

Images download only while the feed card is visible. Downloads are limited to two at a time and 2 MB each, with a small memory-only cache and thumbnails decoded to at most 192 pixels on the longest edge.

Picture Puzzle includes six nature illustrations: Lakeside, Forest, Coast, Mountains, Meadow, and Waterfall. Shuffle changes the sample without immediately repeating it. You can choose a fixed sample or keep using your own picture.

When the puzzle is complete, the pieces gently join into one continuous picture. The finished picture stays until Play again. Animation follows Windows' animation preference and stops safely if you start another game.
