FluentSidebar 1.3.3 — picture puzzle

Picture Puzzle now includes Lakeside, a simple built-in picture. It works offline and is ready to play without choosing a file. Existing custom pictures are kept, and numbered tiles remain available.

The card has a clearer empty slot, consistent tile spacing, and compact controls. The reference button shows the completed picture. Optional number labels help identify picture tiles, and arrow keys move the empty space after a tile has focus.

Finishing the puzzle replaces the tiles with one continuous picture, without gaps or number labels, and shows the move count. The picture stays visible until you choose Play again to shuffle a new, solvable game. Changing pictures keeps your current tile arrangement and move count.

The board fits the card's available width, including the narrow Longhorn rail and custom padding. Image decoding is bounded, the built-in picture is shared between tiles, and moving a tile reuses its existing button.
