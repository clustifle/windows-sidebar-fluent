FluentSidebar 2.0.1

The app launcher opens as a native flyout beside the sidebar. All apps, Pinned, and Windows are available from a compact header, with search below. The grid has consistent tile sizes, clearer labels, and less unused space. Escape or a click outside closes the flyout.

Longhorn Quick Launch has clearer icons, consistent hit targets, and a limited number of rows. Extra pins are available through the overflow entry, without losing their saved order. Right-click a pin to open, reorder, or remove it. Website pins embed their provider favicon on first load and keep it with the pin for offline display. An empty rail offers an entry to choose apps. Icon size and row count are adjustable in Longhorn settings.

The launcher holds auto-hide while open and closes before the sidebar changes sides or theme. Refresh updates the installed-app catalog without restarting the sidebar. Icon decoding remains limited to two requests at a time.
