FluentSidebar 1.3.2 — setup wizard

The installer now offers a full setup wizard with a welcome page, installation-folder selection, desktop-shortcut and startup choices, a review page, installation progress, and an optional launch on completion. Repair and removal are available from the same installer. Startup remains optional, and upgrades preserve its existing state unless another choice is made.

The sidebar now excludes itself from Peek fading and restores its visibility and desktop stacking after shell events such as Show Desktop. Fullscreen applications still take priority. Broader testing is needed for modified taskbars and multiple displays.

This version also includes the app-search placement fix. Notes and preferences are preserved during upgrades.
